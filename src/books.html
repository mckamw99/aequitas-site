About the author

Matthew William McKay, PhD (she/her), is a scholar-practitioner whose research sits where LGBTQ+ identity development meets white racial identity in higher education. Her 2009 master's thesis examined how being outed affects lesbian and gay identity development and introduced the Rainbow Weights and Wings framework. Her 2024 doctoral dissertation at Indiana University named and documented White Rainbow Weaponization: the use of white racial identity to hold power and privilege inside LGBTQ+ spaces. Outed in 2001 and out as trans since 2012, she has spent her career on one question: who controls the story of an identity, and what does it cost when that control is taken? She works in learning analytics and technology, teaches as a community college adjunct, and trains and consults with colleges on racial equity in LGBTQ+ campus communities.
