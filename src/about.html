Our team

Matthew McKay, PhD, CDMP

Principal

Matthew has more than 15 years of experience across enterprise data architecture, business intelligence (BI), and learning and development. He has managed data and BI work at KeyBank and currently leads enterprise learning-management and reporting operations.

He holds a PhD in Urban Education from Indiana University, an MS from Canisius College, and a graduate certificate in nonprofit management from Indiana University. He is a Certified Data Management Professional (CDMP).

Earlier, he served as Director of Student Life & Diversity at SUNY Adirondack. He has taught organizational behavior and leadership as adjunct faculty at Purdue University, the Rochester Institute of Technology, SUNY Adirondack, and Erie Community College.

Jennifer Leon, MA

Consultant, People & Learning

Jennifer has more than eight years in higher education, leading academic support, program management, leadership development, and staff training. She designs workshops built around participation, so audiences practice skills rather than just hear about them.

She holds a master's degree in student affairs in higher education from Indiana University of Pennsylvania, has presented at multiple conferences, and has taught as adjunct faculty at SUNY Adirondack. Her focus areas are leadership, program management, first-year experience, and team effectiveness.
